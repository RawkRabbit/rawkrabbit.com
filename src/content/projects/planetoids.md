## What it does

Fly a small ship through a field of drifting rocks. Thrust, turn and shoot to break them into smaller pieces, and clear the screen before they get you.

Three ways to play:

- **Single player:** chase the high score, with an optional bot wingman.
- **Local co-op:** two ships, one screen, clear the field together.
- **Local versus:** two ships, one screen, and only one walks away.

Each ship has a shield for close calls and a hyperspace jump for when the shield won't cut it. Some rocks fight back: fast ones, explosive ones, magnetic ones that chase you, and golden ones worth chasing.

## Controls

- **Player 1 (blue):** W or Up to thrust, A and D or Left and Right to turn, Space to shoot, S or Down for shield, C for hyperspace.
- **Player 2 (orange):** I to thrust, J and L to turn, Enter or O to shoot, K for shield, U for hyperspace.
- **Touch screens:** on-screen joystick and buttons for one player.
