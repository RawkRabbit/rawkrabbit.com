## What it does

Fly a small ship through a field of drifting rocks. Thrust, turn and shoot to break them into smaller pieces, and clear the screen before they get you.

Three ways to play:

- **Single player:** chase the high score.
- **Local co-op:** two ships, one screen, clear the field together.
- **Local versus:** two ships, one screen, and only one walks away.

Each ship has a shield for close calls and a hyperspace jump for when the shield won't cut it. Player 1 flies blue on WASD or the arrow keys. Player 2 flies orange on IJKL.
