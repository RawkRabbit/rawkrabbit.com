## What it does

Tap the watch screen in time with the track and it shows the BPM. That's the whole app, on purpose.

## What's next

- **Listen mode:** detect the tempo from the music itself, no tapping needed.
- **Gesture tapping:** tap the beat with a finger or thumb gesture instead of the screen.
