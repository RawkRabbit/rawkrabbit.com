## What it does

Pick two bottles and get one page that lines them up: mash bill, age, proof, price and a flavor profile chart, plus a plain-language read on which bottle suits what.

- **Compare any pair:** every combination of the 65 bottles has its own page.
- **Browse the list:** each bottle has a page with its specs and quick links to compare it with others.
- **Random:** jump to a surprise matchup when you don't know where to start.

Specs come from distillery publications and established secondary sources. Anything not officially published is marked as such. Prices are US shelf estimates.

## What's next

More bottles. Each new bottle is one data file, so the list grows without rewriting any comparisons.
