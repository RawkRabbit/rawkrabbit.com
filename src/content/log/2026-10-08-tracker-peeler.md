---
title: Tracker Peeler is live
date: 2026-10-08
summary: A Chrome extension that cleans tracking tags off links and copies them. Free and open source.
project: tracker-peeler
---

Tracker Peeler is live. Click the icon and the current tab's link loses its tracking tags and lands on your clipboard, clean. It also unwraps redirect links and trims Amazon product links down to the essentials.

It's a free download and the code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
