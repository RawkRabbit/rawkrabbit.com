---
title: Hop To It is in beta
date: 2026-10-09
summary: An iPhone app that picks a chore to fit the time and energy you have. Open source, not in the App Store yet.
project: hop-to-it
---

Hop To It is in beta. Tell it you have 30 minutes and low energy, and it picks a chore from your list that fits. It handles repeating chores, understands dictated times like "ten minutes", and answers Siri when you say "I have 30 minutes in Hop To It".

It isn't in the App Store yet. The code is on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone with Xcode can build it today.
